# ADR-0010 — MIT license; single author of record until v0.1.0

Date: 2026-09-24 · Status: accepted

## Context
Maximize adoption and reuse; the maintainer wants sole authorship on commits during bootstrap.

## Decision
MIT. Conventional Commits. No co-author or agent-attribution trailers. English for code, specs and docs; UI strings i18n (en, pt-BR).

## Consequences
+ Permissive for forks and integrations.
− No copyleft protection against closed SaaS forks; accepted.
